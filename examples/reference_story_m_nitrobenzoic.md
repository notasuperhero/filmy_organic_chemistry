# Benzene se m-nitrobenzoic acid kaise banaoge?

*Hand-written reference story showing the target format (not pipeline output).*

## Cast

- **Benzene Haveli and Daadi** [a ring of 6 carbons sharing 6 electrons (aromatic). Very stable, so it swaps groups (substitution) instead of breaking open]
- **Ramu Kaka**, one behind each brother [the H on each ring carbon; it leaves as H⁺]
- **Bade Bhaiya**, who swore never to marry [anhydrous AlCl₃, the catalyst that makes the attacker]
- **Methyl padosi uncle and his three chamche** [–CH₃, sends the next guest to ortho or para; his 3 H are benzylic H]
- **The purple saas, hot mood** [hot KMnO₄]
- **Bade Sahab** [–COOH, sends the next guest to meta]
- **The strict principal and Nitronium** [conc. H₂SO₄ + conc. HNO₃ make NO₂⁺]

## Scene 1: Daadi makes a plan

Daadi: "Humein do mehmaan chahiye, ek doosre se ek seat chhod ke. Par Bade Bhaiya pehle aayenge. Agar warden pehle aa gaya, toh Bhaiya gate se hi laut jaayenge."

[The order of steps matters. Friedel–Crafts does not work on a ring that already has –NO₂, so the carbon group goes on first. The two groups end up meta to each other, so the group on the ring at nitration time must be a meta director.]

## Scene 2: Bade Bhaiya brings Methyl uncle

Bade Bhaiya pulls Methyl away from his clingy partner Chlorine and walks him to the haveli. One second of chaos: one brother holds hands with both Methyl and his Ramu Kaka. Then Ramu Kaka bows: "Main chalta hoon, malik." Bade Bhaiya: "Main sirf rishte karwata hoon." He goes home exactly as he came.

Daadi warns: "Methyl khud host hai, aur mehmaan bulayega. Ghar mein bahut saare benzene rakho."

[AlCl₃ takes Cl from CH₃Cl and makes CH₃⁺, which attacks the ring. The ring loses H⁺ and AlCl₃ is regenerated. The product is more reactive than benzene, so excess benzene is used to limit polysubstitution. Equation: C₆H₆ + CH₃Cl → C₆H₅CH₃ + HCl (anhydrous AlCl₃)]

## Scene 3: The purple saas, hot mood

She sees the three chamche standing beside the haveli and throws all of them out. Methyl uncle comes back in a suit, sanitiser in his pocket. He is now Bade Sahab: "Doori banaye rakhein!"

[Hot KMnO₄ oxidises the side chain to –COOH because it has benzylic H; acid work-up gives benzoic acid. tert-Butyl has no benzylic H and would survive. Equation: C₆H₅CH₃ → C₆H₅COOH (hot KMnO₄, then H⁺)]

## Scene 4: Nitronium is born

The strict principal makes two tough acids fight in a dark gali, and Nitronium is born: "Main sabse pehle!" He barges into the haveli. Bade Sahab stops him: "Ek seat chhod ke baitho." Nitronium sits at C3. Ramu Kaka: "Main chalta hoon, malik."

[Conc. H₂SO₄ turns HNO₃ into NO₂⁺. –COOH pulls electrons out of the ring (deactivating) and sends the attacker to meta. Equation: HNO₃ + 2H₂SO₄ → NO₂⁺ + H₃O⁺ + 2HSO₄⁻; C₆H₅COOH + NO₂⁺ → m-O₂N–C₆H₄–COOH + H⁺]

```
      COOH         m-nitrobenzoic acid
        |
       C1
     /    \
   C6      C2
   |        |
   C5      C3 — NO₂
     \    /
       C4
```

## Scene 5: What if the order is wrong?

If Nitronium comes first, the hostel warden sits down; Bade Bhaiya reaches the gate, sees the warden and turns back. If Nitronium comes while Methyl uncle is still a host, Methyl sends him next door or opposite, and you get the wrong cousins.

[Nitrobenzene does not undergo Friedel–Crafts. Nitrating toluene gives o- and p-nitrotoluene, which oxidise to o- and p-nitrobenzoic acid, not m.]

**Daadi:** "Ghar kabhi nahi tootega. Par jo pehle baitha hai, wahi tay karta hai ki agla kahan baithega."

## Summary

1. C₆H₆ + CH₃Cl → C₆H₅CH₃ + HCl (anhydrous AlCl₃)
2. C₆H₅CH₃ → C₆H₅COOH (hot KMnO₄, then H⁺)
3. C₆H₅COOH + HNO₃ → m-O₂N–C₆H₄–COOH + H₂O (conc. H₂SO₄)
